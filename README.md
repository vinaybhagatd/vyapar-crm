# 🚀 Vyapar Partner WhatsApp & Email Automation Suite v3.16

We are excited to release **v3.16** of the Vyapar Partner WhatsApp Automation Suite! This release introduces robust client-side data portability and automated niche marketing capabilities designed specifically for mobile-first iOS workflows.

## ✨ What's New in v3.16

* **📥 1-Click CSV Lead Export Engine:** Instantly export your entire lead CRM database (including names, businesses, phone numbers, email addresses, niches, status, and follow-up notes) into a clean, spreadsheet-ready `.csv` file saved directly to your local device.
* **📧 Automated Niche Welcome Blog Dispatcher:** Automatically generate and dispatch tailored welcome pitch blogs with your active affiliate referral link (`?referrer_code=ZNNQ2XK`) based on each lead's SME industry (Retail & Kirana, Pharmacies, Wholesalers, or Services & CAs).
* **📸 Enhanced Tesseract.js OCR Parser:** Upgraded visiting card and brochure scanning to automatically extract and sanitize owner names, business titles, phone numbers, and email addresses directly from iPhone camera photos.
* **🎙️ Field-by-Field Voice Wizard:** Capture new leads hands-free while on-site with intelligent speech-to-text parsing.
* **📱 Secure GitHub Pages Deployment:** Fully optimized for iOS Safari with strict HTTPS secure context enforcement for seamless camera and microphone hardware permissions.
